## Style for Rob's personal projects

Applies to personal projects (AI Quick Start, Model Value Map, Your Portable AI Team, new personal sites, handouts). Ensign College work uses the Ensign Career Coach design system instead.

Source of truth: https://github.com/robbagley-afk/ai-expert-toolkit/tree/main/style-guide (README.md, rob-personal.css, tokens.json). Link or copy `rob-personal.css` and its `fonts/` folder rather than restating values. Self-host the fonts; no Google Fonts requests.

- Look: Modernist. Archivo only (400 body, 600 labels, 800 headings and buttons). Ground #f3f2f2, ink #201e1d, muted #605d5d.
- One accent, blue #205bb6. Links and small accent text #174a94. Hover #172441. White on the accent is 6.5:1.
- Blue is a mark: 72px section numbers (01, 02), the period ending a display title, 10px square markers, primary buttons. A full-width blue band holds only large text (40px+).
- Structure: 2px ink rules under the nav, around grids, between equal-width cells and under section heads. 1px #9b9797 only between table rows.
- Square corners everywhere. No shadows, gradients or emoji. Everything flush left, including button labels with a trailing arrow at the far right.
- Body 16px, never under 15px for reading text. Touch targets 44px+, buttons 48px. 2px accent focus outline.
- Charts use their own palette in fixed order: #2a78d6 blue, #eb6834 orange, #1baf7a aqua, #eda100 yellow (dark: #3987e5, #d95926, #199e70, #c98500). Every mark gets a 1px ink outline, a distinct shape, a legend and direct labels. A company keeps its color on every view. A fifth series goes to "Other".
- Print and PDF: same rules at 9–10pt body, white page, one blue band per page at most.
